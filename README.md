### 🎯 About Me

I'm a **2026 B.Tech graduate** who fell in love with the idea that *numbers can tell stories nobody bothers to read* — until someone analyzes them. After finishing my degree, I completed a **Data Analytics certification** to turn that curiosity into a skill set, and I'm now actively looking for opportunities in the **Data Analyst / Data Analytics** space.
